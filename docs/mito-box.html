About the game

Your island, your Mito

Mito Box is a colourful creature-collecting adventure set on an island full of mythic creatures, the Mito. Many are inspired by Indian mythology: Naag the cobra, the great bird Garuda, Airavata, Kamdhenu, Makar, Vanar and more.

Battle wild creatures to weaken them, then throw your capture ball to make them your companions. Captured Mito join your hotbar, follow you around and fight at your side. Some can even carry you: climb onto Garuda and take to the skies.

Between adventures, set up a workbench, unlock new items, craft tools and build your own base from wooden pieces. Pick one of four heroes and explore alone or with a friend on the same Wi-Fi.

Main features

Catch mythic creatures

Weaken a wild creature in battle, then throw your capture ball. The lower its health, the better your chance.

Befriend and team up

Captured creatures join your hotbar. Release them and they follow you, fight beside you and show their affection.

Ride Garuda

Tame the mighty bird of Indian mythology, climb on its back and fly over the island.

Build your own base

Place a workbench, unlock items and craft tools. Build your home piece by piece from floors, walls, doors, windows, stairs and roofs.

Play with a friend

Two-player co-op on the same Wi-Fi network or phone hotspot. No paid server needed.

Four heroes to choose

Start your adventure as Trainer Boy, Trainer Girl, Researcher or Rival.
